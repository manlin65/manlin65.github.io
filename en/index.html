WorkflowPilot · Job Posting Analysis

An n8n workflow compares the requirements of a job posting with a brief profile. Gemini evaluates the match, identifies met and open requirements, and creates an application text draft for high matches.

Workflow in Brief

  • Webhook receives job posting and brief profile.
  • Required fields are checked and the posting analyzed with Gemini.
  • The workflow branches by match score and additionally reviews the draft.
  • Result and draft are returned for human review.
Example: The analysis rates the match as high, marks missing evidence and flags the application text as a draft requiring review.
