SELVAMO AI by manliNova

From Potential to Progress.

SELVAMO AI helps businesses capture processes, evaluate AI opportunities and prepare next steps. Select · Value · Move combines the selection of suitable use cases with transparent financial assumptions.

Who is it for & how does it help?

For small and medium-sized businesses seeking a structured introduction to AI. Benefits, costs and risks inform a prioritised pilot plan.

Three ways to use it

  • Capture business processes and potential AI use cases in a structured way.
  • Evaluate AI ideas against criteria and calculate benefits, payback and ROI using explicit assumptions.
  • Document results and next pilot steps in PDF reports requiring explicit approval.
Development status

Protected pilot workspace with fictional examples. Access by arrangement; external AI is optional and requires consent. The internal agent suitability check is not yet confirmed as available in the online application.

Quoventra AI by manliNova

Find articles. Use stock. Compare offers.

Quoventra AI is a modular procurement assistant that brings together articles, stock levels and supply options. The current demo calculates shortages and compares fictional suppliers by total cost and delivery time.

Who is it for & how does it help?

For automotive workshops and petrol stations with shops. Make better use of existing stock, reduce search effort and prepare traceable purchasing decisions.

Three ways to use it

  • Find articles by part number or search term and check demo stock.
  • Calculate shortages taking existing stock into account.
  • Compare three fictional suppliers using rules for price, shipping, lead time and availability.
Development status

Working demo using local, fictional data in your browser. No live AI integration, automatic web search or real orders. Inventory-system integration is a possible future extension. Do not enter real customer data.
